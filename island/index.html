Driftwood Isle

Island powers

Camp journal

    How to play

    Keep the crew thriving for 10 days. They gather food, haul water, repair shelter and rest on their own. The dogs forage with them. Each power changes conditions everywhere for 16 game seconds. A new power replaces the current weather. Divine energy returns over time. Empty stores drain camp vitality. Reach day 11 to continue in free play.
